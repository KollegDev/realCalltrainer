3. Weitergabe an Google

Für die Sprachausgabe und -erkennung nutzen wir die Gemini API von Google. Deine Audiodaten werden direkt von deinem Gerät an Google übertragen und dort verarbeitet. Eine Übermittlung in Drittländer, insbesondere in die USA, findet statt.

Google agiert dabei nach den eigenen Bedingungen: Gemini API – Nutzungsbedingungen · Google Datenschutzerklärung.

In der kostenlosen Stufe, die dieser Test verwendet, weist Google ausdrücklich aus, dass übermittelte Inhalte zur Verbesserung der Google-Produkte genutzt werden. Menschliche Überprüfung ist dabei nicht ausgeschlossen. Sprich in dieser App daher keine echten Kundennamen, Preise oder andere vertraulichen Informationen aus. Erfinde für die Übung ruhige Beispiele.

3. Transfer to Google

Speech recognition and generation use Google's Gemini API. Your audio goes directly from your device to Google and is processed there, including transfers outside the EU, in particular to the United States.

Google acts under its own terms: Gemini API Terms · Google Privacy Policy.

On the free tier used by this test, Google explicitly states that submitted content is used to improve Google's products, and human review is not excluded. Please do not say real customer names, prices or other confidential information while using the app. Invent your examples.

Voice processing is based solely on your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You give it actively on first launch and can withdraw it at any time by discontinuing use and clearing browser data. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

The random device id is processed under legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, in keeping the test functional and free from abuse.

5. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

In practice: we store nothing permanently that could be linked to you. Everything in your browser you delete yourself. For data held by Google, please contact Google directly.
